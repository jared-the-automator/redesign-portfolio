The patterns

Patterns that work... PERIOD!

Premium stillwater patterns, chironomids, and kits from the BC Interior. No house-brand filler: every pack is built around patterns that catch trout in clear water.

01

Chironomid power packs

80 black bead head pupae in assorted sizes from Bomber 12-2X to 16, or 96 natural-toned pupae for clear water. The midge is the stillwater engine, and this is where the shop leads.

02

Leeches, blobs, and nymphs

The Meat & Potato Stillwater Fly Pack: 52 essential leeches, scuds, blobs, and nymphs for year-round fishing, including UV Straggle Vampire Leeches in high-flash colors.

Custom orders and instruction

Custom orders, pattern advice, and stillwater fly fishing instruction out of the BC Interior. If your water needs a pattern the shop has not tied yet, that is a conversation, not a dead end.
