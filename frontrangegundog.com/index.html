A gun dog shop on the front range, for the people who hunt it

Front Range Gun Dog is a gun dog supply shop in Colorado Springs. The catalog spans training and tracking collars, leads, bird launchers, kennels, containment systems, and the hardware around them.

The store specializes in two things: custom nameplates, and the electronics that run a working dog. Dogtra and Garmin, from the collar on the dog to the GPS unit on the launcher.
