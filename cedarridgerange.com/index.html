The bays

Seven public bays. Walk up and shoot.

Open to the public since 1997. Bays 0 through 5 cover rifles, pistols and shotguns, with a dedicated .22 rimfire bay for practice.

01

Rifle, pistol and shotgun bays

Public bays 0 through 5, with expert staff on hand. Cedar Ridge is well versed in the latest firearms and training tactics, and demands safety so everyone is comfortable on the line.

02

A .22 rimfire bay

A dedicated .22 rimfire bay for new shooters and daily practice, with the 22-rimfire steel bay for target work.

03

Steel challenges

Steel targets on the CRR Steel Challenge range, the Delta Steel Bay and the Mike Bay steel course.

The rental counter goes full-auto.

Try something new from the rental counter, including full-auto and suppressed firearms. Browse firearms and supplies in the gun store, and shop the full inventory online.

  • Machine gun rentals
  • Full-auto and suppressed rentals
  • Silencers and reloading supplies
  • Ammo, firearms and gun storage

Around the range

Hill Country sky, dirt and steel. A look down the public bays.

Shoot after dark.

Low-light night shoots on the range, with night-vision and thermal sessions on the schedule. After dark, the bays go quiet except for the steel.

Bring a light or run it hot. The schedule lives on the range calendar.
