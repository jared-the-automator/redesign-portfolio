EIGHTEEN BAYS IN HUDSONVILLE.

What began as five employees in 1989 is now 186 staff and an eighteen-bay repair facility, with two full-service alignment bays. When the mobile unit can't finish the job, the truck rolls into the shop next door to the parts it needs.

The heavy-duty bench runs on Cummins, Detroit, Paccar, Mercedes and International engines, school and shuttle buses, RVs and motorhomes, cranes, commercial autos and body work.
