No. 04Eighty years

Since 1946, at the same trade

McCoy Millwork opened in Portland in 1946 as a moulding and stair supply house. The racks still hold the same five lines: stair parts, mouldings, mantels, lumber, and exterior. The customers are the cabinet shops, the restorers, and the job sites that have kept the counter busy for eight decades.

Historical renovation work gets its own attention: matching a profile to an existing stair, or finding the right moulding for a room that was finished in the 1920s, is the kind of problem the counter is built for. New construction and home repair come through the same door, with the catalog and the architectural resources on hand to back the order.
