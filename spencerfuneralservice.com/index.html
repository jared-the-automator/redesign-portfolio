Established 1896

Two hearses, four horses, and one address on Broadway

The William F. Spencer Funeral Service was established in 1896. At that time most funerals were held in the home of the deceased, and the business was conducted out of a storefront at 408 E. Broadway. We were the owners of two hearses and four horses that were used on all funerals.

In December of 1946 the operations moved from 408 E. Broadway to our present location at 575 E. Broadway. Funeral customs had changed. It had become the norm to hold the service in the funeral home rather than the residence.

For over a hundred years the William F. Spencer Funeral Service has offered the people of Greater Boston dedicated, reliable and affordable funerals. William F. Spencer, Jr. carries on the tradition that began in 1896.
