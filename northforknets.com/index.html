Built the slow way

Every net starts in one workshop in Ashton, Idaho. No factory, no middle step: raw aluminum goes in, finished nets come out.

Frames are powder coated in nine colors, then fitted with the rubber coated net bag before the whole build leaves the shop.

Anglers send these in all season, from the first warm days to the last run of kings. Every one is a net that did its job.
