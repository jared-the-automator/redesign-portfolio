The survival shelf

Preparedness, in the pages.

The shelf leads with the books people reach for when the lights go out. Practical, unsentimental, and written by people who have read the manuals.

01

What to Expect When the SHTF

An FAQ guide to systemic collapse preparedness. The questions people ask in the first week, answered plainly.

02

Where There Is No Doctor

Field medicine for when the nearest clinic is a long way off. A staple of any preparedness shelf.

03

The Simple Sabotage Field Manual

A U.S. government classic on civil defense, reissued for readers who want the original source.

The literary half

The other half of the shelf.

Between the survival manuals sits the canon. Poetry, essays, and the long letters people keep returning to, printed and bound.

Dylan Thomas. Henry David Thoreau. The autobiography of Mother Jones. The shelf holds the words people reread, right next to the books they need in an emergency.

The shelf, in pictures

A library with a point of view.
