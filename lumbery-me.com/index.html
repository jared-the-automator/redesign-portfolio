The Cedar

Northern white cedar, and the work it does

Northern white cedar runs light yellow, and it grows in New England, the Midwest and southern Canada. It splits clean, weathers well, and has been the New England choice for decks, beds and posts for a long while now.

A small yard in Cape Elizabeth

Lumbery sits on the eastern side of the Portland peninsula, and the cedar comes from small family mills in Maine. Buy local wood: it is cut, stacked and sold by people who will stand behind it.

It is a lumber yard and a garden center under one roof. Bring your cut list, or start with what you have in mind. The hardware, the native plants and the cedar are all here.
