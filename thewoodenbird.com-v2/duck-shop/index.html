Give the commission as a gift

Rick is often booked about six months out. A commission gift certificate lets you give the bird now, even when it cannot arrive in time.

The certificate names the bird you are giving and its estimated arrival date, and explains how the recipient will follow the carving with progress photos. Rick sends a Wooden Bird coffee mug with it, as a stocking stuffer.

How a commission works

  1. Choose your bird

    Tell Rick the species, hen or drake, which way the head should turn, and who the bird is for.

  2. Reserve your place

    A 50% deposit puts you on the schedule, and Rick sends an estimated completion date.

  3. Watch it take shape

    Rick emails photos every couple of days, from the first cut to the last brushstroke.

  4. Welcome it home

    Your bird ships insured, with Rick's signature, a letter about the carving, and a Wooden Bird mug.

Not sure which bird?

Browse the gallery for ideas, then tell Rick what you have in mind. He is happy to talk it through.
