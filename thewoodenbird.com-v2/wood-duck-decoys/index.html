Field notes

Wood Duck

Wood Ducks favor wooded ponds, swamps and slow rivers, and they nest in tree cavities up to 65 feet above the ground. The morning after hatching, the ducklings climb to the cavity opening and jump, clinging with sharp claws and bracing with their tails. The male is colorful for most of the year, and the female shows a white eye patch.

Field notes adapted from the Audubon Field Guide.
