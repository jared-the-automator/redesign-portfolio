Field notes

Northern Pintail

The Northern Pintail is slim and long-necked, and the drake has a white stripe running up his neck onto a brown head, with a long pointed tail. Hens are mottled buff-brown. They summer on prairies, farmland and tundra. Pintail nests sit on dry ground among short plants, and the hen leads her ducklings away within hours of hatching.

Field notes adapted from the Audubon Field Guide.
