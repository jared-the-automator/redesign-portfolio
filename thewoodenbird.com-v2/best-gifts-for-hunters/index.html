Gifts for hunters

A gift for the duck hunter who has everything

Hunters own every decoy a store can sell. A hand-carved bird by an award-winning carver is the one they do not have, and the one they will keep.

Why a Wooden Bird is the right gift

Each piece is decorative art, made to remember good mornings in the blind.

  • Hand-carved by award-winning carver Rick Mignano
  • Looks so real you will want to touch it
  • Fully detailed, textured and wood-burned
  • 50 to 80 hours of work in every bird
  • One of a kind, heirloom quality
  • Many species: loons, mallards, pintails, wood ducks, shovelers and more
