How a season works

Come for a lesson.
Stay for the sport.

The club exists for one reason: to give women's clay target shooting a home of its own, with instruction up front and competition at the end of the season.

01

Instruction first

The club's own mission promises "quality instruction, education, and opportunity for competition." First-time shooters and seasoned competitors work the line together.

02

No gatekeeping

"Everyone is welcome, there is no membership, dues or attendance requirement." One lesson or the whole season. Your call.

03

The Fall Classic

The club's signature competition gets its own page on the site and a dedicated 2025 flyer. It is the event the season builds toward.

The Annies started when a handful of women looked at clay target shooting and saw a gap: the sport needed a home built around it, run by people who already lived it. They formed the club and registered it as a 501(c)(3). Today the club carries instructors, a junior grant, a shop, and the Fall Classic forward from that founding idea.
