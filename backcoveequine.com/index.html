Our services

From routine visits to 2 a.m. emergencies

At Back Cove Equine, we offer a variety of services to care for your horse, donkey, or mule. Three things define the practice.

01

Routine care

Everyday medicine for the animals in your care, with the practice's full range of services on hand when you need them.

02

24-hour emergency

A horse emergency does not keep office hours. The practice's emergency service runs around the clock, so the phone gets answered.

03

Ambulatory visits

The care comes to you. Back Cove Equine is an ambulatory practice, so your animals are seen on their own ground, not in a transport.

Dedicated to compassionate, conscientious care of your equine companion

Back Cove Equine is an equine practice serving Greater Portland, Maine and beyond. The office runs Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., and the practice is built for the moments that fall outside of it.

Horses, donkeys, and mules are all part of the patient list. Most never leave the property they live on.
