Services

A special understanding of horses

Back Cove Equine offers a variety of services to care for your horse, with the work done at your facility across Greater Portland, Maine.

01

24-hour care

When something is urgent, waiting until morning is not an option. The practice provides care around the clock, not just during office hours.

02

Routine and wellness care

Regular checkups and ongoing care keep your horse healthy on the quiet days, so the serious days have a stronger starting point.

03

Ambulatory, at your barn

As an ambulatory practice, the work happens where your horse stands. No stabling, no transport, no stress of a trip to a clinic.

About

An ambulatory practice for Greater Portland

Back Cove Equine is an equine (horses, donkeys, mules) practice serving Greater Portland, Maine and beyond. As an ambulatory practice, the team comes to you, bringing the work to your horse where it stands.
