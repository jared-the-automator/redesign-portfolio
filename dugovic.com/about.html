About

Born in Wells, Maine, Peter Scot Dugovic is a visual artist and sign maker whose work explores the intersection of nostalgia, urban decay, and American iconography. His paintings often feature muted palettes and atmospheric lighting, drawing influence from Edward Hopper's depictions of solitude and Gustav Klimt's ornate patterns.

Trained in both traditional oil painting and commercial sign making, Dugovic blends fine art techniques with the pragmatic precision of the signmaker's craft. His work has been exhibited in galleries across New England and New York, and he has been featured in American Artist Magazine.
