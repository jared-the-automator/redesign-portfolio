About

Born on December 31, 1965, Peter was adopted into a family of nine on a small farm in Honeoye Falls, New York. New York City is where he spent his formative years as a bicycle messenger and sign painter. It was here that he gained an appreciation for gritty urban scenes, wooded areas, and solid endeavors. Those early observations now reflect themselves in most of his paintings. Peter is primarily a self-taught artist, though he studied printmaking and sculpture at both SUNY Geneseo and SUNY Purchase. His education was followed by a move with his family to Kennebunk, Maine, after which he worked as a carpenter and proprietor of his own store. During this time he gravitated back to his artwork, and in 2009 he began his career as a professional artist.

Peter paints from childhood memories as well as from present experiences. His techniques are complex and result in scenes that are compelling and exclusive. Taking time to build his stretchers, stretch canvases, and mix his own paints, when viewing Peter's work one can see the undertone of temperance fueled by passion — a true reflection of himself, visible throughout his work, time and time again.

Peter makes his home in the quiet and secluded back woods of Wells, Maine. He divides his time between his work as a professional artist and carpenter.

The artist has been a part of solo and group exhibitions and has shown in New York City, Long Island, New York; Kennebunk, Maine; and Amesbury, Massachusetts. Private collections include New York, Maryland, California, Connecticut, Georgia, Maine, and Hong Kong.
